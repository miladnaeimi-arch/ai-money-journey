# Sales Call Cheat Sheet

## Discovery Questions

1. How are new leads currently coming into your business?

2. What happens after a new lead comes in?

3. How much of the lead qualification and follow-up process is still manual?

4. Where do leads usually get delayed, missed, or require repetitive work?

5. If we could automate one part of this workflow first, what would save your team the most time?

## Connect the Demo to Their Problem

After the discovery questions, summarize what you heard in one sentence.

Example:

"From what you described, the biggest opportunity seems to be reducing the manual work between a new lead coming in and the first follow-up."

Then show only the parts of the demo that match their problem.

Demo flow:

1. Show a new lead entering the system.
2. Show AI qualification and priority.
3. Show the personalized follow-up being generated.
4. Show the Gmail draft ready for human review.
5. Explain how the workflow could be customized for their lead source and sales process.

Do not show every technical detail.

Focus on:

- time saved
- faster lead response
- fewer missed opportunities
- less repetitive work

## Discussing Price

Do not introduce price before understanding the client's workflow and problem.

After the demo, ask:

"Does this look like something that could help your team?"

If the answer is positive, say:

"Based on what you've described, I can customize this workflow for your business."

For a simple implementation:

"My starter projects typically begin around $500 to $1,000, depending on the workflow and integrations."

For a more customized implementation:

"For a more customized setup with additional integrations and workflow logic, projects typically range from $1,500 to $3,000."

Then ask:

"Would you like me to put together a simple proposal based on your workflow?"

Do not immediately offer a discount.

Focus first on:

- the client's problem
- the value of solving it
- the scope of the implementation
- the next step

## Common Objections

### "It's too expensive."

Say:

"I understand. We can start with a smaller version that focuses only on the highest-value part of the workflow, then expand later if it proves useful."

Do not reduce the price immediately.

### "I need to think about it."

Say:

"Of course. Is there anything specific you're unsure about that I can clarify before you decide?"

Then agree on a follow-up date.

### "Send me more information."

Say:

"Absolutely. I'll send you a short summary of the workflow, what would be customized for your business, and the estimated project range."

Then ask:

"When would be a good time for me to follow up?"

### "We already use automation."

Say:

"That makes sense. I'm not looking to replace what already works. The main question is whether an AI qualification and personalized follow-up layer could improve any part of your existing workflow."

## Main Rule

Do not pressure the client.

The goal of the call is to understand the problem, show relevant value, and agree on a clear next step.
