# AI Sales Automation Offer

## What I Build

I build AI-powered lead follow-up systems for businesses.

The system can:

- analyze incoming leads
- prioritize sales opportunities
- generate personalized follow-up emails
- prepare Gmail drafts automatically
- prevent duplicate drafts
- keep a human review step before emails are sent

## Simple Client Explanation

I help businesses automate repetitive lead follow-up work using AI, so their sales team can respond faster and spend more time on qualified opportunities.

## Ideal Clients

This system is best suited for businesses that:

- receive leads through forms, ads, email, or CRM systems
- manually review and follow up with leads
- have repetitive sales follow-up work
- want faster lead response times
- want AI assistance without automatically sending emails

Potential examples include:

- marketing agencies
- home service businesses
- real estate teams
- B2B service companies
- small sales teams



## What the Client Gets

A customized AI lead follow-up workflow that can include:

- connection to the client's lead source
- AI lead qualification rules customized for the business
- automatic HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW lead routing
- personalized AI follow-up generation
- Gmail draft creation
- duplicate protection
- human review before sending
- setup and testing
- basic workflow documentation

## Current Scope

This offer focuses on lead qualification and follow-up automation.

The current system:

- creates Gmail drafts instead of automatically sending emails

- uses business-defined lead qualification rules

- can be customized for different lead sources and workflows

- is designed to keep a human in control of final outreach

Advanced CRM integrations, dashboards, and multi-step campaigns can be added as separate project upgrades.
