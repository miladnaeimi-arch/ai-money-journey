# Client Delivery Checklist

## 1. Confirm Scope

Before building:

- confirm the exact workflow being delivered
- confirm the lead source
- confirm qualification rules
- confirm required integrations
- confirm what the client will review before sending
- confirm project timeline
- confirm final agreed price

## 2. Build

During implementation:

- connect the lead source
- configure qualification logic
- generate structured AI outputs
- create follow-up workflow
- connect Gmail draft creation
- add duplicate protection
- keep human review before sending

## 3. Testing

Before showing the client:

- test with sample leads
- verify qualification results
- verify lead routing
- verify follow-up generation
- verify Gmail draft creation
- verify duplicate protection
- test at least one failure case
- confirm no email is sent automatically

## 4. Client Review

Walk the client through:

- how leads enter the workflow
- how AI qualification works
- how follow-ups are generated
- where Gmail drafts appear
- how duplicate protection works
- what the client should review before sending

Ask the client to test the workflow with sample data.

## 5. Final Delivery

Before marking the project complete:

- fix agreed issues
- confirm the workflow works as expected
- provide documentation
- explain known limitations
- confirm support expectations
- confirm payment status
- define any future upgrades separately

## Delivery Complete

The project is complete when the agreed scope is working, tested, documented, and accepted by the client.
